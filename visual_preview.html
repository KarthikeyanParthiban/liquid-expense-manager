ACTIVE SELECTION FINTECH DESIGN

Pure OLED Monotone (Option 1)

100% monochrome palette. Maximum focus on amounts with zero color distraction. Perfect for night reading and OLED battery efficiency.

  • Zero Visual Clutter: Eliminates rainbow category badges so only key financial metrics pop.
  • Luminance Hierarchy: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for numbers, silver (`#A3A3A3`) for metadata, graphite (`#121212`) for cards.
  • Inter Typography: Geometric grotesque font with tight number kerning (`-0.8sp`).
💡 UX Tip: Notice how Option 2 introduces subtle micro-semantic tints on currency values without breaking the sleek monochrome background.
